The WP-BPE Algorithmic Family Evolution Information Theory

Mathematical evolution from uncalibrated Pointwise Mutual Information to calibrated, bounded, and length-penalized information gain objectives.

1. WP-BPE Base (Uncalibrated PMI) Vulnerable
$$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv)}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)}$$

Theoretical Flaw: If $u$ and $v$ appear together exactly once in the entire corpus, their joint probability is $1/N$ and denominator is $(1/N)^2$, yielding score $N$. This catastrophic singleton bias merges rare noise before frequent morphemes.

2. WP-BPE FT (Frequency-Thresholded PMI) Calibrated
$$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \begin{cases} \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv)}{\mathrm{freq}(u)^\alpha \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)^\alpha} & \text{if } \mathrm{freq}(uv) \ge \theta \\ -\infty & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

The Fix: Imposes hard frequency cutoffs ($\theta \ge 2$) and alpha-smoothing ($0.75 \le \alpha \le 1.0$) to suppress low-support singletons. Achieves high morphological boundary precision.

3. WP-BPE PIG (Pointwise Information Gain) Optimal LM Quality
$$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot \log_2 \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot N}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)}$$

Information Theoretic Grounding: Weights PMI by candidate frequency, measuring net bits saved across the entire corpus. Dominates downstream proxy-LM bits-per-byte benchmarks.

4. Next-Gen Objectives: SCP, NPMI, and Penalized PIG Gen 2
Symmetric Conditional Probability (SCP):
$$\mathrm{SCP}(u, v) = \frac{P(uv)^2}{P(u)P(v)} = P(u \mid v) \cdot P(v \mid u)$$
Eliminates directional affix bias in agglutinative words.
Normalized PMI (NPMI):
$$\mathrm{NPMI}(u, v) = \frac{\log \frac{P(uv)}{P(u)P(v)}}{-\log P(uv)} \in [-1, 1]$$
Self-normalizing in range [-1, +1] eliminating manual frequency tuning.
Penalized PIG (PPIG):
$$\mathrm{score}(u,v) = \mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot \mathrm{PMI}(u,v) \cdot (|u|+|v|)^{-\beta} \cdot \mathcal{H}(u,v)$$
Combines information gain with length penalty and Markov transition entropy.
