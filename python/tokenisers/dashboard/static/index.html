How the WP-BPE objective evolved

From uncalibrated pointwise mutual information to calibrated, bounded and length-penalised information gain.

  1. WP-BPE Base — uncalibrated PMI Vulnerable
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv)}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)}$$

    The score is at most $1/\max(\mathrm{freq}(u), \mathrm{freq}(v))$, and a pair seen once whose parts occur nowhere else reaches the maximum, 1. This singleton bias merges rare noise before frequent morphemes.

  2. WP-BPE FT — frequency-thresholded PMI Calibrated
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \begin{cases} \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv)}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)} & \text{if } \mathrm{freq}(uv) \ge \theta \\ -\infty & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \quad \theta = 2$$

    A hard frequency cutoff ($\theta = 2$) keeps singleton pairs out of the merge queue; above it the score is the Base ratio.

  3. WP-BPE PIG — pointwise information gain Frequency-weighted
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot \log_2 \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot N}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)}$$

    Weights PMI by candidate frequency: the pair's total pointwise information across the corpus.

  4. Gen 2 — SCP, NPMI and penalised PIG Gen 2
    Symmetric conditional probability
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \frac{(\mathrm{freq}(uv) - d)_+}{\sqrt{\mathrm{freq}(u)\,\mathrm{freq}(v)}}, \quad d = 0.75$$

    Symmetric in $u$ and $v$ and bounded in $[0, 1)$; with $d = 0$ it is $\sqrt{P(u \mid v)\,P(v \mid u)}$. The discount demotes low-count pairs.

    Normalised PMI
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \mathrm{freq}(uv)^{\alpha} \cdot \frac{\ln \frac{P(uv)}{P(u)P(v)}}{-\ln P(uv)}, \quad P(x) = \frac{\mathrm{freq}(x)}{N},\ \alpha = 0.5$$

    The NPMI factor is bounded in [−1, 1]; the support weight $\mathrm{freq}(uv)^\alpha$ keeps rare, perfectly associated pairs from winning outright.

    Penalised PIG
    $$\mathrm{score}(u, v) = \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot \max(0, \mathrm{PMI}(u, v))}{(|u| + |v|)^{\eta}}, \quad \mathrm{PMI}(u, v) = \log_2 \frac{\mathrm{freq}(uv) \cdot N}{\mathrm{freq}(u) \cdot \mathrm{freq}(v)},\ \eta = 0.35$$

    Information gain counting only positive association, divided by a merged-length penalty.
